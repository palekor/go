The Foundry

A vocabulary workshop where every word comes with its sound, its Spanish meaning and clear examples. You hear it, you say it, you use it in a sentence, and the Foundry brings it back at the right moment until it is forged.

  1. Hear everything. Every word and every example has audio, at normal and slow speed. Press play before you read.
  2. Learn the three laws first. Subject, verb, complement. The describing word comes first. Count the beats. Then every word has a place to sit.
  3. Come back and it gets harder, never longer. Help fades as you climb, and a word returns after one day, three, a week and two weeks.
