    The answer tells you which question to ask.

    Before choosing a WH word, identify the missing information: thing, place, time, person, reason, method, amount, choice or owner. Then let the auxiliary carry the time. That sequence — not memorization — is what makes English automatic.

    07 · Coach protocol

    The 60-minute
    premium session.

    One reading per session. Exposure, integration and performance in a single hour — with a written deliverable as homework.

    00 – 10 min

    Exposure

    Perception · Listening

    Listening mode ON. The student hears the passage twice without text and summarizes it in three sentences.

    10 – 25 min

    Integration

    Comprehension

    Text visible, WH lens ON. Complete Understand; the student reads every evidence sentence aloud.

    25 – 40 min

    Retrieval

    Memory

    Question Forge. The coach adds three extra answers from the text; the student builds questions without looking.

    40 – 55 min

    Performance

    Prosody · Speaking

    Record both missions. Feedback on WH accuracy, falling intonation and depth of follow-up questions.

    55 – 60 min

    Commitment

    Deliverable

    Assign the Writing Studio paragraph, sent by WhatsApp before the next session. Close with the Discovery Call.

    Correction protocolWH wordAuxiliarySubjectVerb formWord order

    Never just give the correct version. Name the broken part — “the auxiliary is missing”, “after did, base verb” — and let the student repair it. Self-correction builds the engine; correction from outside only builds dependence.
